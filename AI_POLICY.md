# Generative AI / LLM Policy

Out of respect for Lily Foote and the original Django Rusty Templates project, please see [upstream's AI/LLM policy](https://github.com/LilyFirefly/django-rusty-templates/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md) for Lily's stance.

Django Rusty Templates (Agentic) welcomes AI-assisted and agentic development. Our policy is to stay within the terms of the existing [BSD-3-Clause license](LICENSE), including its copyright, attribution, redistribution, and non-endorsement requirements. Contributions must be compatible with that license.
